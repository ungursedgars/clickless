2. Two roles: controller and processor

Depending on the situation, our role under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) differs.

  • We are the controller of the data you give us when you contact us, request a demo or sign an agreement (name, email, phone number, company details).
  • We are the processor of the documents that the client and the client’s employees send to the bot (invoices, receipts, Z reports, booking data). The controller of this data is the client. Processing takes place under a data processing agreement signed with each client.

4. How client documents are processed

  1. The client sends a photo or PDF of a document to the bot in Telegram.
  2. The bot passes the file to Google Gemini to read the data (supplier, number, date, amounts).
  3. The bot saves the file and the data it read in a folder created by the bot in the client’s own Google Drive.

Documents are processed only during this process. We don’t make copies in our own systems, and our staff don’t open client documents. The exception is when the client asks for help with a specific document.

5. Google user data

The service requests access to Google Drive with the drive.file scope. It lets the bot create and edit only the files and folders the bot created itself. The bot cannot access any other files in your Google Drive.

We use Google user data only to save the client’s documents and tables in the client’s Google Drive. We don’t sell it, don’t use it for advertising, don’t pass it to third parties (except the processors listed in point 6 that are needed to provide the service), and don’t use it to train AI models.

CLICKLESS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time on your Google account page myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Security

  • Access to our systems is protected with two-factor authentication.
  • The Telegram connection to the bot is verified with a secret key.
  • Document contents are not written to technical logs.
  • Every action of the bot is logged in the client’s own Google Drive, so the client can see what the bot has done.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct or delete it, restrict or object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. Write to info@clickless.lv, and we will reply within one month.

If you believe your data is processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia, www.dvi.gov.lv.

If you are a client’s employee or another person whose data appears in a client’s documents, please contact the client first, as they are the controller of that data.

The clickless.lv website does not use cookies or visitor analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
