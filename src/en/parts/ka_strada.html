How it works

Three steps, and we do two of them

  1. A short call

    In 15 minutes we find out what documents you have, how many companies, and who needs to get the reports. We show you how the bot works with a real invoice.

  2. We set it up

    We set up the bot, the folders in your Google Drive and the tables within one day. You don’t have to configure anything.

  3. You send a document

    Take a photo of a paper receipt or forward a PDF invoice from email to the bot. The bot reads the data, saves the file in the right month folder and adds it to the table.
