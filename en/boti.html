Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most often

How does the free first month work?

We set up the bot and you use it free for a month. Nothing to sign and no card needed. A week before the end, the bot asks whether you want to continue and shows the exact amount. The setup and monthly fees start only if you agree. If not, you owe nothing, and all files stay in your Google Drive.

Does it replace accounting software?

No. The bot collects and organises documents so that they reach your accountant complete and on time. Your accountant keeps working in their own software, for example Jumis or Horizon.

What documents can I send?

Invoices, delivery notes, expense receipts and cash register Z reports. Take a photo of a paper document, or forward an electronic invoice from email to the bot as a PDF. If one PDF holds several invoices, the bot splits them.

Does it meet the State Revenue Service (VID) requirements?

For cash registers with an electronic control unit, a cash register journal is not mandatory. Cabinet Regulation No. 96 requires printed Z reports to be pasted into a Z report register showing the date and serial number (point 56.1), and you keep doing that as before. From a photo of the Z report, the bot builds an organised table that your accountant uses for bookkeeping, and keeps copies of the Z reports in your Google Drive. Nothing has to be retyped by hand any more.

What if the AI reads something wrong?

The bot always shows the data it read, and you can correct it with a button. If the amount without VAT, the VAT and the total don’t add up, the bot warns you. The original photo or PDF is always saved next to it.

How many documents can I send?

The monthly fee includes up to 500 documents. If you need more, each further 500 cost 5 €. Most small companies need 50 to 200.

Can employees send documents too?

Yes. With the Employee access add-on, each employee has their own access, and you see who sent what.

What happens if I stop using the service?

All folders, files and tables stay in your Google Drive. The bot stops working, and your documents stay with you.

Do you work with accountants?

Yes. If you are an accountant and your clients would benefit from organised documents, contact us about working together.
